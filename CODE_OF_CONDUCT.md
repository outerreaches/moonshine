# Code of conduct

Moonshine contributors and maintainers are expected to make participation
welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free.

Examples of positive behavior include constructive technical disagreement,
clear attribution, patient review, and respect for different experience
levels. Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discriminatory language,
personal attacks, deliberate intimidation, doxxing, and publishing private
information without permission.

This policy applies in repository issues, pull requests, discussions, review
channels, and public spaces where someone is representing the project.
Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and may temporarily or
permanently restrict participation when conduct is inappropriate.

Report conduct issues privately to a repository owner using the contact
details on their GitHub profile. Reports will be handled as confidentially as
practical. Good-faith reports will not result in retaliation.

This policy is informed by the
[Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/),
version 2.1.
